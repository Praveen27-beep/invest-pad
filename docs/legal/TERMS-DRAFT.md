1. **What Invest-pad is.** A research and note-keeping tool for your own investment

   security, does not suggest position sizes or price targets, and is not an investment
   adviser or research analyst service. Alerts compare your holdings with limits you set

3. **AI output can be wrong.** Answers are generated by a third-party model from your
