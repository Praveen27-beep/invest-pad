How it works

Collect

Upload annual reports and transcripts (PDF, Excel, Word). Write linked notes. Save passages from any web page with the Chrome side panel.

Reason

Write your thesis and the assumptions under it. Mark which rest on a source and which are your judgement. Attach evidence for and against.

Decide

Record each decision with the one reason that decided it and what would change your mind. Records are kept as written; changes are new versions you can compare.

Come back

Open the company and the overview shows the decision, what it rested on, how each assumption stands now, and what has been added since.

What's in it

  • Ask questions of your own material; every statement quotes its source, and statements whose quote can't be found are dropped
  • Ask “why did I buy this?” and get your own decision back, cited
  • Check a new document against your assumptions; suggestions only, you accept or dismiss
  • “How I invest”: write your rules; decisions are checked against them as questions, never verdicts
  • Red-flag and forensic-accounting checklists you can edit
  • Ratios with the formula shown: DuPont, cash conversion cycle, Beneish M-score, accruals and more
  • Import holdings; set your own limits (for example, no position above 10%) and see when one is crossed
  • A monthly review that asks hard questions from what you've recorded
  • A decision journal across companies and a graph of how your notes, documents and assumptions connect
  • Use your own model and key (OpenAI-compatible endpoints, including local ones), with cost caps
  • Read-only access from Claude and other MCP clients

Your data stays yours

Separated by the database

Each account's data is isolated by row-level security in the database itself, and tested on every change.

Export everything

One click gives a ZIP of everything you own — notes, versions, theses, decisions, answers, your original files.

Delete means delete

Deleting a document removes its text and anything derived from it. Deleting your account removes all of it.

Not advice

Invest-pad is a research tool. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security, or how much. Alerts compare your holdings with limits you set; review questions are prompts for your own thinking. Model answers can be wrong — check the cited source.
